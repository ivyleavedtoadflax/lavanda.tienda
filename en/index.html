A farm in Osset

Osset is a hamlet of Andilla, in la Serranía: hills of pine and limestone, a good hour from Valencia. Here the lavender grows in rows, between terraces and scrub.

No warehouses or shops in between. You deal with the grower, and the lavender goes from the farm to you.
